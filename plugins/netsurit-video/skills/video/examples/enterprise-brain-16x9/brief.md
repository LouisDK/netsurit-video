# The Enterprise Brain: "Words, Numbers, Beliefs"

**Spec:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 170.5 s (voice-led), infographic style, narrated by George (ElevenLabs, eleven_multilingual_v2), score composed in code under the voice.
**Story:** `_bmad-output/story-2026-10-10.md` (Sophia). **Format:** a morph chain timed to the voice: 8 eras, 7 shape morphs, 0 hard cuts.
**Device:** the company's head starts hollow. The Librarian and the Clerk each fail to fill it. It turns saffron when it keeps beliefs, and ends full with both of them plugged in.
**Colour:** saffron = memory/belief only. Periwinkle = the Librarian. Indigo/navy = the Clerk and structure. Teal = emphasis.

## Sound
Thin pad and plucks through the keepers, with a deflating figure on "both weigh the same". A row-click motif for the Clerk. Hard stop after t2 (≥1.5 s near-silence), then the loudest hit (stab + sub) when the head opens, before "That's what an enterprise brain keeps". A rising-fifth motif for the brain. The tick motif in era 6 stops on "nothing at all". The fullest section is the computation (b9). A warm final chord, then a fade.
